Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about flooding and our website.

Flooding can be caused by heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers, poor drainage systems, and rapid urbanization.

Prepare an emergency kit, keep important documents safe, follow weather updates, and know your evacuation routes.

Move to higher ground, follow official evacuation instructions, avoid walking or driving through floodwater, and stay updated through reliable sources.

An emergency kit should include drinking water, food, a flashlight, batteries, a first-aid kit, medicines, important documents, and a phone charger.

Flood awareness helps people understand the risks, prepare in advance, protect their property, and reduce the danger to their lives.

INFINYX is a Flood Awareness website designed to raise awareness about flooding, explain its causes and consequences, and teach people how to prepare for floods.
