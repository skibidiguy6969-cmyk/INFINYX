Floods Can Change Lives

Floods can damage homes, roads, schools, and farmland. They can also interrupt daily life and put communities at risk. Understanding flood safety is an important step toward reducing these dangers.

By learning about flood risks and preparing early, we can make better decisions and help protect the people around us.
